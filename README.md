# CARDIO-ML-ED — Clinical ECG Research Dataset

**Research contribution · Published dataset · September 2026**

CARDIO-ML-ED is a multimodal dataset bringing together ECG recordings in image format and structured clinical information. The published dataset describes records from **574 patients** and was collected from routine clinical workflows at two academic medical centers.

**Official dataset:** [Mendeley Data — CARDIO-ML-ED, version 2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/zfszfmpb82/2)  
**DOI:** [10.17632/zfszfmpb82.2](https://doi.org/10.17632/zfszfmpb82.2)  
**Published:** 6 September 2026  
**Dataset license:** CC BY 4.0 (see the official Mendeley record)

## My involvement

I am listed among the contributors to the published dataset. During the research project, my work included collecting and scanning ECG images, organizing and curating records, supporting validation, and contributing to associated software development. This page describes my research participation; it is **not** a claim that I independently created or published the entire dataset.

## About the dataset

The Mendeley release describes:

- ECG images paired with structured clinical information.
- Demographic and clinical data, including vital signs, laboratory results, medical history, and patient outcomes.
- Annotations described by the publishers as based on dual-expert consensus.
- **574 patient records in version 2.** Earlier project figures may differ from the final published release.

For the authoritative dataset description, complete contributor list, access information, and license, consult the [official Mendeley Data record](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/zfszfmpb82/2).
